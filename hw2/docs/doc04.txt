The Solar System

Our solar system consists of the Sun and all the objects that orbit it. The eight
planets, in order from the Sun, are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, and Neptune. Each planet has unique characteristics.

Mercury is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun, with surface temperatures
ranging from extremely cold to extremely hot. Venus has a thick atmosphere of carbon
dioxide, making it the hottest planet. Earth is the only known planet with liquid
water on its surface and life.

Mars, the red planet, has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.
Jupiter is the largest planet, with its famous Great Red Spot storm. Saturn is known
for its beautiful ring system made of ice and rock. Beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt,
a region of icy objects including the dwarf planet Pluto.
