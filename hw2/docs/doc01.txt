The Red Fox and the Hound

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The fox was known throughout the forest
for its cunning nature and swift speed. Every morning the fox would dash through the
meadows, leaping over streams and darting between trees.

The hound, loyal and persistent, always chased the fox but could never quite catch it.
The dog had powerful legs and an excellent nose, yet the fox outsmarted the hound
every single time. The fox knew every hidden path and secret burrow in the forest.
