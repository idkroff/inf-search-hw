Animal Kingdom: Mammals and Birds

Mammals are warm-blooded vertebrates characterized by the presence of mammary glands,
hair or fur, and live birth (with the exception of monotremes). They include diverse
groups such as carnivores, herbivores, and omnivores.

The wolf is a highly social predator that lives and hunts in packs. Wolves communicate
through howling, body language, and scent marking. The dog is a domesticated descendant
of the wolf and has been a companion to humans for thousands of years.

Birds are warm-blooded vertebrates with feathers, wings, and beaks. Most birds can fly,
though some species like penguins and ostriches cannot. Eagles are powerful birds of
prey known for their keen eyesight and aerial hunting skills.

The fox belongs to the family Canidae, along with wolves, dogs, and jackals. Foxes are
highly adaptable animals found on every continent except Antarctica. They are known for
their intelligence, agility, and diverse diet including small mammals, birds, and berries.
