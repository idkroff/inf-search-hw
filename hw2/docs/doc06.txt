Climate Change and Global Warming

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns.
Since the industrial revolution, human activities have been the main driver of climate
change, primarily due to burning fossil fuels like coal, oil, and natural gas.

Rising global temperatures cause glaciers and polar ice caps to melt, leading to rising
sea levels that threaten coastal communities. Extreme weather events such as hurricanes,
droughts, and floods are becoming more frequent and intense.

Carbon dioxide and methane are the primary greenhouse gases responsible for warming.
Renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power can reduce
carbon emissions. Governments worldwide are adopting policies to limit warming to
1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels, as outlined in the Paris Agreement.
